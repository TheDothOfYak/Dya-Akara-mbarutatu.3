# 4 Brothers World: versions

## v1.6
- **Acorn World rebuilt, and much bigger.** The ground sits lower, so the sky has room for enormous trees. West to east: the Green Sea, the Keepwood and the Keilia keep, Capstead, the Green Deep, the Tangle (where the Elsha'ryn hide), the Canopy and the King's Clearing. Block changes from the old Acorn World are set aside (still saved, just not used), because they would land in the wrong places. Your hero, bag, buddy and boss kills are all kept.
- **Capstead has two home trees,** each one giant tree three times the old size: a twenty-block trunk of bark with a staircase inside climbing seven floors, houses out on the branches, a cellar under the west tree, and a crown of thick branches. Rope bridges join the two trees, ladders run up past the branch houses with trapdoors at each porch, and a climbing rope hangs from the low bridge. On the ground: the plaza with Pebble the Stryx fountain, Old Ruut's Punk shed, and Orrin's stone cottage.
- **The Keilia keep** on the west side. Its four towers are trees, joined by stone walls with a wall walk. It's friendly (Master Brakka, Dunvarr the smith, Captain Ferrow and the guards), but the Archive, the Treasury, the Master's quarters, the walls, the tower tops and the Undercroft are off limits. Guards walk those places; if one sees you (watch its sight cone), you're marched out of the gate. Three puzzles open the vaults: the levers under the four banners in the Hall of Hammers, the rune stones in the Archive, and a beacon lever at the top of the tallest tower that opens the Deep Vault far below. Plus a bookcase that isn't one. Lots of chests.
- **The Memory Grove of the Elsha'ryn,** hidden under a thick roof of leaves in the Tangle: grown houses of living wood, a library tree with memory cores to listen to, Lethra (who trades) and Phorus the Kalo'Eik.
- **The Duskareth camp** in the root caves under the Green Deep: raiders with knives and slingers who throw them, hide tents, and their plunder. (These Duskareth can't touch the Duat. They only wish they could.)
- **The creatures of Torcain's Run** now live in Acorn World (and only there): Wild Punks, Domestic Punks, Malsti Punks (they steal from your bag: catch them to get it back), Kofi Galta and Big Momma Kofi, RubberMcFly, Albali Byrds (their horns paralyse you, then the film heals you) and toxic Villtur Albali, Hvaleia swimming through the air above the sea, Tonguatjis, Kipsu packs, Uff, Mikolo Moko, Makari swarms and the Lutut. Never hurt a RubberMcFly: the ShurgrEdan answers.
- **Stryx live only in Acorn World** now.
- **New blocks:** ladders, trapdoors (R opens and shuts them), rope bridges, climbing ropes and wood stairs you can walk up, all craftable, plus new gear from the new creatures (Albali Salve, Kipsu Hood, Scute Mail, Lutut Lance, Hvaleia Bow).
- Trees whose trunks are several blocks wide now look like one tree, not a row of thin ones.
- **Back to Dya'Akara** button on the title screen.

## v1.5
- **Joining works on any network.** When two computers can't link up directly (school or work wifi, phone data, strict routers), the game now connects through a relay automatically: the same Supabase realtime service Dya'Akara's online rooms use. A direct link is always tried first; if it hasn't come up after about 4 seconds the relay takes over, and the status line says "Connected through the relay". Nothing to set up. (The relay needs the Dya'Akara site's settings, so it works when you play from the website, not from a downloaded copy of the game.)
- **Victory!** Beat Apophis, then use the rift that opens where it fell: a celebration screen with fireworks, a fanfare, all four heroes, everyone who beat the Duat with you, and your legendary reward. From there you can travel on to any world.

## v1.4
- **Drop and destroy items.** Press **Z** to drop what you're holding on the ground (hold Shift for the whole stack). In the bag, Shift-click a slot to drop its whole stack, or pick an item up and press **Drop** (on the ground) or **Destroy** (gone for good; it asks twice). A dropped item waits about a second before you can pick it up again, so walk away from it if you want to leave it. Dropped items are only on your own screen, so to hand something to a brother use **X**.
- **Relay for networks that can't link up.** If a brother's wifi, phone data or router won't let two computers connect directly, the game can now pass the connection through a relay server, but only when the direct route fails. The relay is off until you add one: see "Relay for tricky networks" in HOW-TO-ADD.md. Nothing changes for players whose networks already work.

## v1.3
- **Multiplayer fixes.** Everyone in a room must be on the same version, and the game now says so plainly instead of breaking ("The host is on version 1.2.1 and you are on 1.3: both of you open the same link and refresh"). Joining tells you whether the room code was wrong, the room server couldn't be reached, or your two networks won't link up. The host reconnects by itself after a sleep or wifi blip, more connection helpers are used, the multiplayer add-on has a backup download, and a block or monster from a newer version is skipped instead of crashing the game.
- **Worlds.** Fuzzy World's Tall Peaks are one broad range of rounded mountains, not spikes, and the seams between areas no longer leave spikes or cliffs. Underground there are real caves: big rounded caverns joined by wide tunnels, instead of a sponge of little holes. More water veins everywhere, with crystals growing along their floors. In Rock World the Heart Chamber (the Gemscale Leviathan) is now in the middle of the planet's inner layer, wrapped in water tubes, and flooded to the roof; the Leviathan swims and lunges through it. The worlds are made fresh from this version, so blocks you changed before stay changed but the ground around them may be different.
- **Torches** can be placed on the ground or on a wall (click while holding one).
- **Water** looks like water: a moving, wavy surface, deeper colour the deeper it goes, falling streams, and crystals and plants that sit under it. A small pocket of water with nothing under it now falls and spreads (seas and lakes stay put).
- **Less blocky.** Ground has rounded corners and uneven edges, grass has real blades that droop over edges, and blocks have more detail (pebbles, cracks, roots, shells, the odd fossil) and twice the variety. Grass and flowers never float: they go when the ground under them does.
- **Pause menu** (Esc or the Menu button): back to the game, travel, settings, controls, the 4 Brothers World home screen, or back to Dya'Akara. On your own the world stops while it's open.
- **Travel back** to any world you've already been to, from anywhere (pause menu or map). New worlds still need a rift or an altar.
- **Armor.** Helmets and body armor from hide, fish scales, iron, bronze, platinum and stygian. Right-click to wear it; your armor shows on your hero and to other players, and soaks up part of every hit.
- **Gold crystals are magic.** Each one teaches you a new elemental spell (fire, then lightning, then poison, then more), makes all your magic stronger and gives you more mana, as well as making you bigger.
- **More customizing.** Every hero can wear a hat (cap, crown, flower crown, wizard hat, bandana, bow) and a cape. Fuzzies don't have ears.
- **Playing together:** other players see your armor, your hat and cape, your acorn buddy, your arrows and stones in flight, and your fancy weapons (fire, poison, lightning, crystal and legendary weapons glow). Press **X** to give what you're holding to the player next to you.
- **New font** that's easier to read (VT323), with arcade titles (Press Start 2P).
- The title screen no longer says "New Rock", and the creator and death screens match the hero you picked.

## v1.2.1
- The game is now called **4 Brothers World**.
- **Torches.** Make 4 from 1 wood and 1 flint (or a ship plank and flint, or 2 cave moss and flint). Hold one like anything else, or press **T** to carry one in your other hand while you use your sword, pick or bow. Water puts it out.
- **Stryx** live in the forests, mountains and jungles of Fuzzy World and Acorn World: wide, flat slabs of stepped stone with vines draped over their backs. They're chill, but stand on one (or hit it) and its vines come up lashing until it calms down.
- **Settings** (O, or the button on the title screen): full screen, turn the scroll wheel round, change any control (every key and mouse button, two keys each), and save your progress to a file or load it back.
- Gnarlroot only reaches about 22 blocks from its trunk now. Back off further and it waits.
- Chopping a tree only fells that tree, not the ones whose leaves touch it.
- The acorn buddy double jumps, so it keeps up when you climb, and pops back to you if it ever gets stuck far behind.
- Leaves look like leaves: puffy pixel clumps, lit from above, with round, bumpy edges.
- Jumping costs less stamina (2, double jump 3). Every crystal you eat adds 2 max stamina (gold adds 5), counting the ones you've already eaten.
- Running is a real run now, nearly twice as fast as walking, and keeps its speed through a jump.
- Your own glow and the cube lantern light up caves again (lights were being dropped every frame).
- Updates keep your progress: the first time a new version runs it also keeps a spare copy of your old save.

## v1.2
- **E** opens your bag. **R** now talks to people, reads carvings and uses altars and rifts.
- Swords, picks and bows sit in your hero's hand and swing from it.
- Heroes look the way they're walking: their face turns left and right.
- Water flows: dig into a lake or pour water off a ledge and it runs down and spreads out instead of hanging in the air. Still lakes stay still.
- Flint and gravel poke out of the ground near every starting village, and flint now also shows up in dirt, so you can always make your first pick.
- Full screen button on the title screen and in the top-right corner.
- Text boxes and in-game text always stay on screen and wrap on small windows.
- Stamina: a bigger bar, and 20% more stamina.

## v1.1
- New look for the fuzzy hero: a fluffy scalloped outline, smooth shading, bigger shiny eyes, rosy cheeks, round paws.
- New look for the acorn hero: a real acorn shape that tapers to a point, a scaly cap with a rim and stem, twig arms, little shoes.
- "Make your hero": the species buttons are just Fuzzy, Rock, Acorn and Ghost.
- The version number shows on the title screen.

## v1.0
- First release as The 4 Brothers Worlds.
- Four home worlds (Fuzzy, Acorn, Rock, Ghost) and four playable heroes.
- The Duat: the co-op world that opens once a fuzzy, a rock, an acorn and a ghost have each beaten their home world's boss. Stamina, soul drain, Great World Feeders, shades, fanatics and the priests of the Gate.
- The final boss: the possessed reflections, which fuse into Apophis. Legendary gear for each hero. The sixth world is a locked teaser.
- Everyone in a world fights the same monsters and bosses.
- Room codes: play together outside Claude. One player hosts, the others join with the code.
