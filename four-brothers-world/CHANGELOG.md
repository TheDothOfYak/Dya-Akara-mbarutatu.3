# 4 Brothers World: versions

## v1.8
Everything here is in Acorn World.
- **Your buddy picks things up.** When there's nothing to fight, your acorn buddy runs over to anything lying on the ground nearby (and to fallen Albali fruit) and brings it to you. It leaves alone anything you threw down yourself.
- **Waystones.** Nine waystones along the road: the keep, the traveller's hut, the Gorge, the old shrine, Capstead, the Green Deep, the Memory Grove, the wreck and the King's Clearing. Press R on one to wake it; press R on a woken one to travel to any other you've woken.
- **Beds and campsites.** Sleep in a bed or rest by a campfire (R or right-click) to heal, and you'll wake up there instead of at the start after you fall. Beds are in some of the branch houses and the traveller's hut, and you can make your own (4 planks, 3 Kofi hair).
- **A house of your own.** Bring Orrin 30 planks and the little house at the very top of the east tree is yours: a bed, a storage chest, a banner and farm soil by the window.
- **Albali Aagac trees** in the Green Deep, with pale leaves. Their fruit drops now and then: pick it up to eat, or leave it on the ground and it hatches into an Albali Byrd.
- **Market day.** Every third day Senna the Kalo trader sets up a stall in the plaza by the west tree. What she sells changes every market. She wants pearls, and for some reason old boots.
- **Night in Capstead.** Old Ruut, Brannoc and Cubert go indoors at night, and glowing RubberMcFly drift round the crowns of the home trees.
- **The Sunear'Zikhron.** On some days, late in the afternoon, the Waves of Memory roll in: the sky turns violet, light falls in bands, echoes of acorns long gone walk past, and a pale eye drifts across the land. Stand in the eye for 20 seconds to hear a memory (and get a crystal), once per storm.
- **Rain and fog.** Some mornings it rains over the Green Deep, the Tangle and the Canopy, and the Gorge river rises three blocks until it stops. The Megla Wood is always foggy now.
- **The Kipsu den.** A shaft in the Broken Hills drops into a warm hollow where a Kipsu pup has been left behind. Its pack may be home. Talk to the pup and take it home.
- **The Su Naga.** Rarely, deep in the Green Sea, a three-headed sea serpent rises. Each head spits gas that poisons you. Take two heads and the last one (the first head, the oldest) takes only a quarter of the damage. It drops Naga scales, a gold crystal, and sometimes the Naga Trident.
- **Pets.** Hold a creature's favourite food and right-click it, and after a try or two it's yours: Kofi Galta love berries, Big Momma Kofi love Kofi grass, Mikolo Moko love Uff petals and Domestic Punks love living vines. One pet at a time; it follows you everywhere in Acorn World. Right-click your pet to say hello; a pet Punk carries saddlebags (15 slots). RubberMcFly belong to nobody.
- **Fishing.** Make a fishing rod (4 wood, 2 living vines), hold it and right-click water to cast. When the bobber dips and you see a "!", right-click to reel in. Sea Perch, Glowfin, pearls and Krabbi shells in the sea, river trout in the Gorge, carp in ponds and cave pools, and old boots everywhere. Nutmeg has a new job: three carp for supper.
- **Farming.** Berries, Uff petals and Kofi grass can be planted on farm soil and grow back when you harvest them. Farm soil can now be made from 2 dirt and a berry.
- **Orrin's building contest.** Build anything between the stakes east of Capstead, then ask Orrin to judge it. He scores size and how many different blocks you used, with bronze, silver and gold prizes.
- **Build over leaves.** Leaves, tree trunks, tall grass, flowers and hanging moss are background: place a block right over them.
- **Walk in front of chests and workbenches** (and every crafting station). They're furniture now, not walls. Guards still can't see through a chest, so they still hide you in the keep.
- **Brighter trees, flickering light.** The home trees, the keep's tower trees and the library tree have wall torches on every floor, Capstead, the keep and the grove are lit a little brighter, and lanterns, torches, campfires and forges flicker.
- Fixed: night never actually came for the creatures. Things that only come out at night (Malsti Punks, Makari, the Lutut), bigger night spawns and the guards' shorter sight at night now really happen at night.

## v1.7
- **The wreck of the Ember.** An Eldi fire-tree ship lies crashed nose-first in the Canopy, east of the Tangle. Climb the rope over its side or up through the breach in its belly: a hold full of cargo, the captain's cabin with the ship's log, and the cannon on deck. The Pilot Stryx still sits in its stump at the bow, waiting for orders. Find three Eldi seeds for it, then load one into the cannon (R) and fire a last salute.
- **More life in the water.** The Green Sea is much deeper, with kelp and stone coral on the sea floor and on the floor of the Gorge river, sunken chests and a sunken rowboat. New creatures: Raf Krabbi (armoured crabs that walk the sea floor and shock you if you bother them), Su Grothyn (glowing cone shells that whip their vine at anything that pokes them) and Harkal (lantern sharks that hunt).
- **Jobs at the keep.** Master Brakka, Captain Ferrow and Dunvarr the smith have jobs now, including sneaking jobs: put a borrowed plan back on the Archive shelf, or reach the top of the tallest tower, without a guard seeing you. Get caught and the job's off until you ask again.
- **The memory core hunt.** Lethra asks you to find eight memory cores scattered across Acorn World (the hills, the bottom of the Gorge, the Megla Wood, the Duskareth camp, the keep, the wreck, a deep cave and the Canopy). Each tells part of the fall of Aakalay; bring them all home and she shows you the whole story, and gives you the Memory Circlet. Phorus has a smaller job in the library tree.
- **Grow-forging.** The Elsha'ryn have a growing altar in the Memory Grove. Stand by it to grow weapons and armour that keep the nature of what they grew from: the Thornroot Spear (poison), the Emberwood Bow (fire), the Stormbark Staff (lightning), the Shockshell Club and Livingwood Mail.
- **Catch with Pebble.** Ask Brannoc to play: Pebble flings mud balls for 30 seconds and you catch as many as you can. Better scores win better prizes, and there's a gold crystal for a really good one.
- **Chests open.** Press R or right-click any chest to look inside: click something to take it, click something in your bag to put it in, or Take everything. A treasure chest fills with its loot the first time you open it. New Storage Chest to keep your own things in (6 planks and an iron bar at a workbench). Break a chest and whatever is inside falls out. What's in a chest is kept in your own browser, so in a shared game each player has their own chests.
- **Gold crystals never go to waste.** Once you've eaten the most your body can hold (8), each extra gold crystal melts into pure stamina: +10 max stamina and a full refill, up to 25 more crystals (+250 stamina). On every world.
- **A map you can zoom.** Scroll or use + and − to zoom (it zooms toward your cursor), drag to look around, "Me" to jump back to yourself and "Whole map" to see everything. It opens zoomed in on where you are, and places you've explored are labelled.
- Pressing R now uses whatever is nearest: a carving or memory core right next to a door no longer opens the door instead.

## v1.6
- **Acorn World rebuilt, and three times wider (3,300 blocks).** Getting anywhere is a journey now. The ground sits lower, so the sky has room for enormous trees. West to east: the Green Sea, the Keepwood and the Keilia keep, the Broken Hills (steep ridges, a traveller's hut halfway), the Gorge (a deep river crossed by an old Keilia rope bridge with gaps in it: jump them, or fall in, float, and climb the ropes back up), the Megla Wood (dark, crowded pines and a ruined shrine), Capstead, the Green Deep, the Tangle (where the Elsha'ryn hide), the Canopy and the King's Clearing at the far east end. Waystones along the road say how far you have to go. The keep is a long walk west of Capstead, the King a long walk east. Block changes from the old Acorn World are set aside (still saved, just not used), because they would land in the wrong places. Your hero, bag, buddy and boss kills are all kept.
- **Capstead has two home trees,** each one giant tree three times the old size: a twenty-block trunk of bark with a staircase inside climbing seven floors, houses out on the branches, a cellar under the west tree, and a crown of thick branches. Rope bridges join the two trees, ladders run up past the branch houses with trapdoors at each porch, and a climbing rope hangs from the low bridge. On the ground: the plaza with Pebble the Stryx fountain, Old Ruut's Punk shed, and Orrin's stone cottage.
- **The Keilia keep** on the west side. Its four towers are trees, joined by stone walls with a wall walk. It's friendly (Master Brakka, Dunvarr the smith, Captain Ferrow and the guards), but the Archive, the Treasury, the Master's quarters, the walls, the tower tops and the Undercroft are off limits. Guards walk those places; if one sees you (watch its sight cone), you're marched out of the gate. Three puzzles open the vaults: the levers under the four banners in the Hall of Hammers, the rune stones in the Archive, and a beacon lever at the top of the tallest tower that opens the Deep Vault far below. Plus a bookcase that isn't one. Lots of chests.
- **The Memory Grove of the Elsha'ryn,** hidden under a thick roof of leaves in the Tangle: grown houses of living wood, a library tree with memory cores to listen to, Lethra (who trades) and Phorus the Kalo'Eik.
- **The Duskareth camp** in the root caves under the Green Deep: raiders with knives and slingers who throw them, hide tents, and their plunder. (These Duskareth can't touch the Duat. They only wish they could.)
- **The creatures of Torcain's Run** now live in Acorn World (and only there): Wild Punks, Domestic Punks, Malsti Punks (they steal from your bag: catch them to get it back), Kofi Galta and Big Momma Kofi, RubberMcFly, Albali Byrds (their horns paralyse you, then the film heals you) and toxic Villtur Albali, Hvaleia whales in the sea and the Gorge river (they come up to spout), Tonguatjis, Kipsu packs, Uff, Mikolo Moko, Makari swarms and the Lutut. Never hurt a RubberMcFly: the ShurgrEdan answers.
- **Stryx live only in Acorn World** now.
- **New blocks:** ladders, trapdoors (R opens and shuts them), rope bridges, climbing ropes and wood stairs you can walk up, all craftable, plus new gear from the new creatures (Albali Salve, Kipsu Hood, Scute Mail, Lutut Lance, Hvaleia Bow).
- Trees whose trunks are several blocks wide now look like one tree, not a row of thin ones.
- **Armor and capes on acorns, redrawn.** Helmets fit over the cap with plumes, crests or horns (each material has its own look); body armor adds shoulder plates, a collar, a belt and tassets and never covers the face; capes hang from the shoulders, stream out behind when you run and lift when you jump.
- **Armor looks.** Under your armor in the bag, pick a Helmet style (plume, crest, horns, wings, spike or plain), an Armor style (plates, scales, studded, sash or fur trim) and a Colour. They only change how it looks, they're saved with your hero, and other players see them.
- **Doors that work.** Houses, the home trees, the keep's towers and the Hall of Hammers have real doors. Walk into one and it opens; it shuts behind you once you're clear (never on anyone standing in it). Press R or right-click to open or shut one yourself and leave it that way. Make your own: 4 planks make 3 door blocks (stack three).
- **Trapdoors open by themselves** when you climb a ladder up into one, or stand on one and hold S, and shut again once you've gone. R and right-click work on them, on levers and on rune stones too.
- **Guards can't see through things.** Walls, shut doors and trapdoors, chests, shelves and any other solid block hide you, and the sight cone on screen stops where their view does.
- **Back to Dya'Akara** button on the title screen.
- **Jobs in Capstead.** The village's Eikar give you little quests: Old Ruut, Brannoc, and three new villagers, Nutmeg the cook (east tree), Pip (west tree) and Hazel the lookout (top of the west tree). Each has a short chain: bring things, chase off creatures, go and see something, or visit a place, and one of Hazel's only opens after the Warthog King falls. A yellow ! marks someone with a new job, a green ? one ready to hand in, and your current jobs show under your health bars.

## v1.5
- **Joining works on any network.** When two computers can't link up directly (school or work wifi, phone data, strict routers), the game now connects through a relay automatically: the same Supabase realtime service Dya'Akara's online rooms use. A direct link is always tried first; if it hasn't come up after about 4 seconds the relay takes over, and the status line says "Connected through the relay". Nothing to set up. (The relay needs the Dya'Akara site's settings, so it works when you play from the website, not from a downloaded copy of the game.)
- **Victory!** Beat Apophis, then use the rift that opens where it fell: a celebration screen with fireworks, a fanfare, all four heroes, everyone who beat the Duat with you, and your legendary reward. From there you can travel on to any world.

## v1.4
- **Drop and destroy items.** Press **Z** to drop what you're holding on the ground (hold Shift for the whole stack). In the bag, Shift-click a slot to drop its whole stack, or pick an item up and press **Drop** (on the ground) or **Destroy** (gone for good; it asks twice). A dropped item waits about a second before you can pick it up again, so walk away from it if you want to leave it. Dropped items are only on your own screen, so to hand something to a brother use **X**.
- **Relay for networks that can't link up.** If a brother's wifi, phone data or router won't let two computers connect directly, the game can now pass the connection through a relay server, but only when the direct route fails. The relay is off until you add one: see "Relay for tricky networks" in HOW-TO-ADD.md. Nothing changes for players whose networks already work.

## v1.3
- **Multiplayer fixes.** Everyone in a room must be on the same version, and the game now says so plainly instead of breaking ("The host is on version 1.2.1 and you are on 1.3: both of you open the same link and refresh"). Joining tells you whether the room code was wrong, the room server couldn't be reached, or your two networks won't link up. The host reconnects by itself after a sleep or wifi blip, more connection helpers are used, the multiplayer add-on has a backup download, and a block or monster from a newer version is skipped instead of crashing the game.
- **Worlds.** Fuzzy World's Tall Peaks are one broad range of rounded mountains, not spikes, and the seams between areas no longer leave spikes or cliffs. Underground there are real caves: big rounded caverns joined by wide tunnels, instead of a sponge of little holes. More water veins everywhere, with crystals growing along their floors. In Rock World the Heart Chamber (the Gemscale Leviathan) is now in the middle of the planet's inner layer, wrapped in water tubes, and flooded to the roof; the Leviathan swims and lunges through it. The worlds are made fresh from this version, so blocks you changed before stay changed but the ground around them may be different.
- **Torches** can be placed on the ground or on a wall (click while holding one).
- **Water** looks like water: a moving, wavy surface, deeper colour the deeper it goes, falling streams, and crystals and plants that sit under it. A small pocket of water with nothing under it now falls and spreads (seas and lakes stay put).
- **Less blocky.** Ground has rounded corners and uneven edges, grass has real blades that droop over edges, and blocks have more detail (pebbles, cracks, roots, shells, the odd fossil) and twice the variety. Grass and flowers never float: they go when the ground under them does.
- **Pause menu** (Esc or the Menu button): back to the game, travel, settings, controls, the 4 Brothers World home screen, or back to Dya'Akara. On your own the world stops while it's open.
- **Travel back** to any world you've already been to, from anywhere (pause menu or map). New worlds still need a rift or an altar.
- **Armor.** Helmets and body armor from hide, fish scales, iron, bronze, platinum and stygian. Right-click to wear it; your armor shows on your hero and to other players, and soaks up part of every hit.
- **Gold crystals are magic.** Each one teaches you a new elemental spell (fire, then lightning, then poison, then more), makes all your magic stronger and gives you more mana, as well as making you bigger.
- **More customizing.** Every hero can wear a hat (cap, crown, flower crown, wizard hat, bandana, bow) and a cape. Fuzzies don't have ears.
- **Playing together:** other players see your armor, your hat and cape, your acorn buddy, your arrows and stones in flight, and your fancy weapons (fire, poison, lightning, crystal and legendary weapons glow). Press **X** to give what you're holding to the player next to you.
- **New font** that's easier to read (VT323), with arcade titles (Press Start 2P).
- The title screen no longer says "New Rock", and the creator and death screens match the hero you picked.

## v1.2.1
- The game is now called **4 Brothers World**.
- **Torches.** Make 4 from 1 wood and 1 flint (or a ship plank and flint, or 2 cave moss and flint). Hold one like anything else, or press **T** to carry one in your other hand while you use your sword, pick or bow. Water puts it out.
- **Stryx** live in the forests, mountains and jungles of Fuzzy World and Acorn World: wide, flat slabs of stepped stone with vines draped over their backs. They're chill, but stand on one (or hit it) and its vines come up lashing until it calms down.
- **Settings** (O, or the button on the title screen): full screen, turn the scroll wheel round, change any control (every key and mouse button, two keys each), and save your progress to a file or load it back.
- Gnarlroot only reaches about 22 blocks from its trunk now. Back off further and it waits.
- Chopping a tree only fells that tree, not the ones whose leaves touch it.
- The acorn buddy double jumps, so it keeps up when you climb, and pops back to you if it ever gets stuck far behind.
- Leaves look like leaves: puffy pixel clumps, lit from above, with round, bumpy edges.
- Jumping costs less stamina (2, double jump 3). Every crystal you eat adds 2 max stamina (gold adds 5), counting the ones you've already eaten.
- Running is a real run now, nearly twice as fast as walking, and keeps its speed through a jump.
- Your own glow and the cube lantern light up caves again (lights were being dropped every frame).
- Updates keep your progress: the first time a new version runs it also keeps a spare copy of your old save.

## v1.2
- **E** opens your bag. **R** now talks to people, reads carvings and uses altars and rifts.
- Swords, picks and bows sit in your hero's hand and swing from it.
- Heroes look the way they're walking: their face turns left and right.
- Water flows: dig into a lake or pour water off a ledge and it runs down and spreads out instead of hanging in the air. Still lakes stay still.
- Flint and gravel poke out of the ground near every starting village, and flint now also shows up in dirt, so you can always make your first pick.
- Full screen button on the title screen and in the top-right corner.
- Text boxes and in-game text always stay on screen and wrap on small windows.
- Stamina: a bigger bar, and 20% more stamina.

## v1.0
- First release as The 4 Brothers Worlds.
- Four home worlds (Fuzzy, Acorn, Rock, Ghost) and four playable heroes.
- The Duat: the co-op world that opens once a fuzzy, a rock, an acorn and a ghost have each beaten their home world's boss. Stamina, soul drain, Great World Feeders, shades, fanatics and the priests of the Gate.
- The final boss: the possessed reflections, which fuse into Apophis. Legendary gear for each hero. The sixth world is a locked teaser.
- Everyone in a world fights the same monsters and bosses.
- Room codes: play together outside Claude. One player hosts, the others join with the code.
