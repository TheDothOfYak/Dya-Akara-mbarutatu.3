# V2 preview

## Heroes
- **Ghost:** a cloud blob that fades in and out. **V** turns you invisible for 6 s: monsters lose track of you, and your first hit from hiding lands twice as hard (16 s cooldown).
- **Rock:** no spells. Eat fire, lightning or poison crystals and they grow on your body, up to 5 of each. Your punches carry that element, **F** slams the ground and **Q** switches crystals. Your hits smash dirt and stone 3–5 blocks deep.
- **Fuzzy:** drawn half again as big, with his original look, and turns side-on to run.

## Monsters
- Ghosts are cloud blobs. Each area holds a set number of them. Clear them and it stays quiet for about 7 minutes.
- Rare elites that take some thinking. Only one shows up at a time, and not often.
  - Ghost Tank and Lütut: Ghost World and the Duat.
  - Thornback: Fuzzy World.
  - Canopy Stalker: Acorn World.
  - Geode Golem: Rock World.
- Cyitors, the Empire's elite peacekeepers.

## Worlds
- Fuzzy World's mountains are broad slopes instead of spires.
- Its caves join up through long tunnels.
- Lava pools in the mountain caves and a magma chamber under the peaks.
