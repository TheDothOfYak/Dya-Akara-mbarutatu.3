# 4 Brothers World — V2 (preview)

The big redesign, kept separate from the main game so we can always go back.
Play it from the **Play V2** button on the Flake Games home page, or at
https://thedothofyak.github.io/Dya-Akara-mbarutatu.3/four-brothers-world-v2/

- `index.html` is the main game (v1.8) with V2's changes, and loads `v2.js` (heroes, ghosts, elites, Cyitors) and `v2s2.js` (hero magic, staffs, coins, the trader, legendary chests, recipe squares), `v2s3.js` (town portals), `v2s4.js` (how each hero moves, swimming and air), `v2s5.js` (living trees, and the rock's building fix), `v2s6.js` (every hero runs in a full side view), `v2s7.js` (the Warthog King can't be fought from the cliff any more), `v2s8.js` (the bigger Ghost World and its creatures), `v2s9.js` (the fuzzy's puff jumps, and how trees are drawn), `v2s10.js` (the acorn's vine), `v2s11.js` (how each hero mines), `v2s12.js` (fewer recipes; Recipe Squares teach the rest), `v2s13.js` (building in shapes, pouring sand, cracks that stay), `v2s14.js` (the Duat Stone), `v2s15.js` (runes instead of picks, and the Ancient Rune Keeper), `v2s16.js` (busier Fuzzy World caves, more chests, fall damage for everyone), `v2s17.js` (the acorn's roll, the ghost's wall slip, and wind) `v2s18.js` (the Geodling, a little geode guy in Rock World) `v2s19.js` (the fuzzy's weather magic: gust, rain, storm, whirlwind and hail, and how they combine), `v2s20.js` (Tanglehold, the jungle village), `v2s21.js` (back hits, dazing, and bumps in beats) `v2s22.js` (ridgebacks, shellbacks, the centipede bats, Captain Shellbrace, and Tanglehold's defenders) and `v2s23.js` (the wider Fuzzy World: the Deep Tanglewood, the waterfall, the Woolly Highlands and Curlmanes, home-trees, scorpion dens and the Mother Scorpion, tame cavalry animals, fireflies and sunbeams), `v2s24.js` (themed caves, flowing lava, clams) `v2s25.js` (weather, sunrises and sunsets, a kinder wind, rupees) `v2s26.js` (the Stormmark lightning power, chests by hand, the dirt wall fix) `v2s27.js` (golden chests by the bosses, silver chests in the scorpion dens) and `v2s28.js` (Cloudtop, the land on the cloud above Tuftwood). Elite tiers live in `v2.js`; elite loot, coins and prices in `v2s2.js`.
- V2 saves to your account separately from the main game (`fbw2:` rows, `rw2_` in the browser), and has its own multiplayer rooms. Everyone playing together needs to be on V2.

See CHANGELOG.md for what's in it, and CLAUDE.md for a map of how the code fits together.
