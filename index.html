Travel through the Duat

Other worlds flicker on the far side

Each boss that falls opens the way to the next world: Fuzzy World, then Acorn World, then Rock World, then the Ghost World. When a fuzzy, a rock, an acorn and a ghost have each beaten their home world, the Duat opens for everyone. Any Duat Altar takes you to worlds you have opened.
